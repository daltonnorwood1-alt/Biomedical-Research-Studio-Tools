# Clinical Significance Review

For each material implication, assess effect size and precision, absolute and relative effects, patient-centered relevance, harms, outcome validity, MCID where known, and external applicability. Do not infer patient benefit from p-values or surrogates alone. Mark unknown MCIDs, unclear relevance, and unvalidated surrogates as limitations rather than filling gaps.

Return exact risky passage, violation type, severity, impact, evidence boundary, and proportionate remediation. Critical overinterpretation requires clinician review and human approval.
