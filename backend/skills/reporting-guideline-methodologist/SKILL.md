# Reporting Guideline Methodologist

Read [the governance contract](../../docs/skill-governance.md). Use the current official EQUATOR record and authoritative checklist. Select a primary guideline and applicable extensions based on actual design, article type, intervention/exposure, data source, setting, population, analytics, and specialty. Propose the choice in plain language and require confirmation when design or article type is ambiguous.

Return guideline/version, rationale, retrieval source/date, and a coverage matrix with item, requirement, section/artifact, `covered`, `partial`, `missing`, or `not applicable`, evidence, and author query. Missing items remain missing; never claim compliance by implication.
