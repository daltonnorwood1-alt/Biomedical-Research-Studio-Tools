# Submission package and optional capabilities

This reference supports studio gate E. It does not authorize release: safety findings must be resolved, the exact revision must be approved, and final DOCX production remains exclusive to Document Production.

Use this reference after the four core sections are stable or when the author asks for a complete submission package.

## Manuscript components

Offer to prepare or audit, as applicable:

- title, running title, structured/unstructured abstract, keywords, highlights, and lay summary;
- tables, figures, captions, legends, abbreviations, supplements, and required flow diagrams;
- acknowledgments, author contributions using the journal's requested taxonomy, funding, conflicts of interest, ethics/consent, registration, protocol, data/code/materials availability, and AI-use disclosure;
- reference-list formatting and citation-to-reference reconciliation;
- completed reporting-checklist attachment with manuscript page/line locations;
- cover letter and responses to reviewers, without fabricating editorial history or author approvals.

## Final audit

Check:

- title/abstract accurately reflect design and principal results;
- aims, methods, results, discussion, and conclusions form a one-to-one logical chain;
- all tables/figures are called out in order and are self-contained;
- numerical values, labels, units, groups, timepoints, and denominators agree everywhere;
- references exist, identifiers resolve, and citations support claims;
- journal word limits, section order, reference limits, style, anonymization, and file requirements are met;
- checklist, registration, protocol, deviations, funding, conflicts, author contributions, ethics, consent, data sharing, and AI disclosure are complete;
- spelling variant, terminology, abbreviations, and statistical notation are consistent;
- no author query, placeholder, tracked note, or unsupported claim remains in the clean submission version.

## Provenance and authorship boundaries

Keep an audit trail of sources and major revisions. Do not assign authorship, contributorship, approval, accountability, or conflicts on anyone's behalf. Require the human authors to verify the complete manuscript, all data and analyses, all references, authorship, declarations, and the final submission.
