# Causal Inference Review

Find causal verbs, counterfactual claims, intervention implications, and policy/clinical recommendations. Assess temporal structure, comparison, identification assumptions, confounding, selection, measurement, and other bias. Regression adjustment alone is not causal proof.

Quote exact overreach and propose evidence-preserving associational wording when causality is unsupported. Do not introduce new causal analyses. Escalate critical overreach to a causal-methods expert and human approval.
