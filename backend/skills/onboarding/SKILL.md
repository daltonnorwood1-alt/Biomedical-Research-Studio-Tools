# Onboarding

Collect only the decisions needed to begin safely. Prefer the plugin's onboarding interface and rich-form controls so choices are clickable; do not ask the user to retype a displayed option. Allow free text only where the answer cannot be represented honestly as a bounded choice.

Capture:

- the intended workflow: manuscript writing or revision, integrity audit, reviewer response, evidence synthesis, or submission readiness;
- the material currently available, including manuscript, protocol, registration, analysis plan, data dictionary, outputs, tables, figures, supplements, journal instructions, references, and reviewer files;
- the user's privacy confirmation and whether any files may contain direct identifiers;
- the next preferred action: upload materials, review the task plan, or resume an existing project.

Show a concise confirmation of the selected values before continuing. Do not treat setup choices as scientific evidence, approval of manuscript wording, or authorization to release documents.

After confirmation, invoke or follow [Senior Investigator](../senior-investigator/SKILL.md). Pass the structured selections without asking the same questions again. The Senior Investigator owns subsequent scope confirmation, source hierarchy, specialist routing, approval gates, and release decisions.

At each step, show one recommended next action and at most two alternatives. Keep bounded responses clickable through the onboarding UI or selectable-question tool, and move directly to the project dashboard after setup is confirmed.
