# Submission Readiness

Verify within a clearly stated scope: manuscript components; title page and declarations; reporting checklists; current journal requirements; file names; tables, figures, and supplements; privacy; citation state; open findings; approvals; and release manifest.

Report each item as satisfied, partial, missing, not applicable, or not assessable with evidence location. Do not assert journal acceptance, regulatory compliance, or readiness outside the assessed scope. Any safety `FAIL`, unresolved critical/major finding, missing gate, or privacy quarantine blocks release.

Read [the governance contract](../../docs/skill-governance.md).
