# Statistical Methods Review

Assess estimand, analysis population, outcome/model compatibility, effects and uncertainty, denominators, missing data, multiplicity, clustering, repeated measures, confounding, assumptions, diagnostics, and reproducibility. Cross-check Methods, outputs, tables/figures, and Results.

Do not introduce analyses merely to repair prose. Identify absent output and complex or unassessable analysis for human statistical review. Reject proof language, “trend toward significance,” non-significance as evidence of no effect, and p-value-only reporting where effect and precision are applicable.
