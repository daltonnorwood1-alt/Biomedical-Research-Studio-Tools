# Privacy and Deidentification

Classify sensitivity before downstream processing. Screen locally for direct identifiers and unnecessary patient-level detail. Quarantine flagged sources; do not reproduce identifier values in findings, logs, prompts, or final artifacts. Request a deidentified replacement or an explicit authorized disposition.

Use the minimum content necessary for each bounded task. A privacy safety failure remains unresolved until a human decision and verification are recorded. Report what was screened, detection limitations, withheld context, and the exact required action.
