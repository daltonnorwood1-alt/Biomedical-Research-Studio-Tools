Results

Among 1,284 participants included in the primary analysis, 642 were assigned to the intervention group and 637 to the comparator group. Median follow-up was 18.4 months.

The primary outcome occurred in 184 participants in the intervention group and 216 in the comparator group (adjusted hazard ratio, 0.82; 95% confidence interval, 0.68 to 0.98).

Secondary outcomes

No material difference was observed in all-cause mortality. Hospitalization was lower in the intervention group, although the analysis was not adjusted for multiplicity.

The treatment effect was consistent across prespecified subgroups. The interaction P value for age was 0.05.
