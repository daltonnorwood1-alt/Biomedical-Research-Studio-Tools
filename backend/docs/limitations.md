# Limitations and Deferred Capabilities

## Implemented

- Local SQLite state and immutable hashed upload storage.
- Direct-identifier screening and quarantine.
- Versioned records, deterministic finding IDs, audit hash chain, task and approval services.
- Five safety-rule definitions and deterministic acceptance checks.
- Approval-gated document production with authentic WordprocessingML revisions attributed to Dalton Norwood.
- Accessible dashboard, intake, task, evidence, approval, and artifact views.
- Senior Investigator and 16 bounded specialist skills, including living literature evidence synthesis.

## Deferred

- Full arbitrary-DOCX round-trip preservation, including complex tables, fields, floating drawings, footnotes, headers, section properties, and pre-existing revisions.
- Production PubMed, Crossref, EQUATOR, journal, EndNote, RIS, BibTeX, and Google Scholar integrations.
- Full manuscript parsing, citation-to-claim linking, reporting-checklist engines, and response-letter generation.
- Visual DOCX rendering through a Word-compatible renderer on hosts where one is available.

The supplied audit builder is integrated with additional path, size, content-type, and CSP controls. Its archived MIT declaration did not include a `LICENSE` file, so redistribution licensing remains unresolved.

Deferred items are not silently simulated. The release manifest must continue to state scope and limitations.
