Support

For installation problems, workflow questions, or suspected defects, open an issue in the Biomedical Research Studio Tools issue tracker.

Describe the affected workflow, the plugin version, what you expected, and what happened. The issue tracker is public. Do not include patient identifiers, protected health information, access tokens, passwords, manuscript content, unpublished results, confidential reviewer material, or other sensitive data.

Privacy questions

For questions about this plugin’s own data practices, open a non-sensitive issue and label the title “Privacy question.” The skills-only plugin does not give its publisher access to ChatGPT conversations or uploaded files. Requests concerning ChatGPT account data or conversation deletion must use OpenAI’s account and privacy controls.

Security reports

Do not disclose credentials, exploit details, or sensitive research material in a public issue. Open a minimal issue stating that you need a private security-reporting route, without including the sensitive details.

Response scope

Support covers the Biomedical Research Studio plugin package and its included workflows. It does not provide patient-specific medical advice, legal advice, institutional privacy approval, or emergency support.
