Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 5, 2026

Scope

This policy covers the public, skills-only edition of the Biomedical Research Studio plugin for ChatGPT. The plugin provides static workflow instructions for biomedical manuscript writing, evidence review, research-integrity auditing, and related scholarly tasks. This edition does not connect to a publisher-operated MCP server, user account system, database, analytics service, or advertising service.

Information handled by the publisher

The plugin publisher does not receive, collect, or store users’ ChatGPT prompts, uploaded files, manuscript content, research data, generated responses, account identifiers, or usage history through this skills-only plugin. The plugin package contains instructions and supporting reference files; it does not transmit content to infrastructure operated by the publisher.

ChatGPT and third-party processing

Content that users provide in ChatGPT is processed by OpenAI under the terms and privacy settings applicable to the user’s ChatGPT account. When a user explicitly requests an external literature search or opens a third-party source, OpenAI-provided capabilities and the selected source may process the submitted search terms under their own policies. Users should use privacy-safe queries and must not place patient identifiers, credentials, confidential manuscript passages, or row-level sensitive research data into external searches.

Website data

This policy page is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web-request information, such as an IP address, browser details, and access logs, under GitHub’s own privacy statement. The plugin publisher does not add cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or profiling scripts to this site.

Use, sharing, and sale

Because the publisher does not receive plugin conversation or file content, the publisher does not use, sell, rent, or share that content. The plugin contains no advertising and does not perform behavioral profiling.

Retention and deletion

The publisher retains no ChatGPT conversation or uploaded-file data through this skills-only plugin. Users manage ChatGPT conversations, uploaded files, retention settings, and deletion requests through the controls and privacy processes provided by OpenAI. GitHub retains website request data according to GitHub’s policies.

Sensitive information

Biomedical Research Studio is not a HIPAA-covered data service or a substitute for institutional privacy review. Do not provide direct patient identifiers, protected health information, account credentials, or material you are not authorized to process. The included privacy-screening workflows are safeguards and do not guarantee de-identification or legal compliance.

Future changes

If a future release adds a hosted MCP server, external storage, authentication, or publisher-operated data processing, this policy will be updated before that functionality is made available.

Questions

For non-sensitive privacy or support questions, use the contact route on the support page. Do not include patient information, credentials, manuscript content, or confidential research data in a support request.
