Privacy Policy

Effective: October 5, 2026

Biomedical Research Studio processes information that users deliberately provide to perform manuscript, evidence, auditing, and document workflows. This may include project metadata, manuscripts, research tables, figures, references, and generated workflow records.

Account and isolation

The hosted service requires user authentication. Each authenticated identity is assigned a separate data store and artifact directory. Access tokens are validated but are not intentionally written to application data or logs.

Use of information

Information is used only to provide requested Studio functions, maintain workflow provenance, enforce approval and safety controls, diagnose failures, and protect the service from abuse. The service does not sell user content.

Sensitive information

Users must not upload identifiable patient information, credentials, or information they are not authorized to process. Identifier screening is a safeguard, not a guarantee of de-identification or regulatory compliance.

Storage and deletion

Project information and generated artifacts remain on the hosted service until deleted under the service’s retention process or removed by an administrator after a verified request. Infrastructure backups may persist for a limited operational period. Public launch requires documented retention and deletion procedures from the hosting operator.

Third parties

The service may contact PubMed/NCBI and Crossref for literature metadata and citation-health checks. Hosting and authentication providers process limited technical information needed to operate the service. ChatGPT processes conversations and tool calls under the terms governing the user’s ChatGPT account.

Contact

For privacy or deletion requests, use the contact method on the support page.
